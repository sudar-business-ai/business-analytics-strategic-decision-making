## Overview
Organisations increasingly rely on business analytics to make strategic decisions in complex
and uncertain environments. This research examines how **data quality**, **analytics capability**
and **managerial support** influence the adoption of business analytics and the effectiveness of
strategic decision-making in data-driven organisations.

## Research Objectives
1. Evaluate the relationship between data quality and strategic decision-making effectiveness
2. Assess how analytics capability influences the accuracy and timeliness of strategic decisions
3. Determine the effect of managerial support on the adoption of business analytics
4. Examine the combined impact of all three factors on strategic decision-making performance

## Theoretical Framework
- **Information Processing Theory (IPT):** better data and analytics improve an organisation's ability to reduce uncertainty
- **Resource-Based View (RBV):** data, analytics and leadership act as valuable strategic resources

## Methodology
| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Research design | Quantitative, cross-sectional survey |
| Philosophy | Positivist, deductive approach |
| Sample | 111 professionals across retail, IT, manufacturing and other sectors |
| Instrument | 25-item questionnaire on a 5-point Likert scale (pilot tested) |
| Data collection | Online survey distributed via LinkedIn and professional networks |
| Analysis tool | IBM SPSS |
| Techniques | Descriptive statistics, Cronbach's alpha reliability, Pearson correlation, multiple regression |

All measurement scales showed excellent reliability (Cronbach's α = 0.942 – 0.965).

## Key Findings
| Hypothesis | Result | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| H1: Data quality → Decision effectiveness | r = 0.828, p < 0.001 | ✅ Supported |
| H2: Analytics capability → Decision effectiveness | r = 0.854, p < 0.001 | ✅ Supported |
| H3: Managerial support → Analytics adoption | β = 0.470, p < 0.001 | ✅ Supported |
| H4: Analytics capability → Analytics adoption | β = 0.493, p < 0.001 | ✅ Supported |
| H5: Combined effect → Decision effectiveness | R² = 0.764, p < 0.001 | ✅ Supported |

- Managerial support and analytics capability together explain **88.7%** of the variance in business analytics adoption.
- Data quality, analytics capability and managerial support together explain **76.4%** of the variance in strategic decision-making effectiveness.
- No single factor dominates: the best decisions come from **balanced investment** in data, analytics skills and leadership.

## Recommendations for Organisations
- Treat **data quality** as a strategic asset through ongoing data governance and quality metrics
- Build **analytics capability** holistically: tools, cloud infrastructure, hiring and upskilling
- Ensure **visible leadership sponsorship** of evidence-based decision-making

## Limitations and Future Research
- Cross-sectional design limits causal conclusions; longitudinal studies are recommended
- Sample skewed towards younger and male respondents in retail and IT sectors
- Future work could use mixed methods and cross-industry or cross-country comparisons
